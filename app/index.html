Roadmap to the platinum

Take the phases in order. Story first — nothing there needs grinding. Then strangers and side missions, then activities, then the long collectible and count grinds, with 100% completion last, since it feeds on almost everything else.

Missable & story-locked trophies

Most story trophies are not missable — earlier GTA games let you replay missions, and nothing in the official descriptions says otherwise. The cautious short list below covers trophies tied to a one-time moment, a partner being present, or a condition that is hard to repeat. Do the partner trophies the first time the story pairs you with your partner.
