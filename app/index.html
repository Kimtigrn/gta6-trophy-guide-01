Roadmap to the platinum

Work the phases in order. Story first (nothing there needs grinding), strangers and side missions second, then activities, then the long collectible and count grinds, and 100% completion last — it feeds on almost everything else.

Missable & story-locked trophies

Most story trophies are not missable — prior GTA games let you replay missions, and nothing in the official descriptions says otherwise. The short list below is the cautious one: trophies tied to a one-time moment, a partner being present, or a condition that is hard to repeat. Do the partner trophies the first time the story pairs you with your partner.
