The road to the platinum

Five phases, in order. Story first — it needs no grinding at all. Then the strangers and side missions, then activities, then the long collectible and counting sweeps, with 100% completion last because it feeds on almost everything else. Each phase card shows the next trophy in that phase that you still need.

Missable & story-locked trophies

Most story trophies are not missable — earlier GTA games let you replay missions, and nothing in the official descriptions says otherwise. The short list below is the cautious one: trophies tied to a one-time moment, to your partner being beside you, or to a condition that is hard to repeat. Do the partner trophies the first time the story puts you together.
